Shelley Sunjka grew up in South Africa — a place that teaches you to be direct, to read a room, and to mean what you say. She brought that instinct across the world to Ireland, where she built a career as a copywriter and marketing strategist for founders who are tired of sounding like everyone else.

"Your message is your most powerful asset. Not your logo. Not your colour palette. Your words."

She is a member of The Copy Posse — Alex Cattoni's global community of storytellers and copywriters. She brings full-funnel thinking, brand voice strategy, and campaign architecture to small but mighty brands who are doing something that actually matters.

Her clients include animal rescue nonprofits, female-founded consultancies, and mission-driven service businesses who needed words that could carry the weight of what they believe.
